We would like to work with the Adult dataset that aims to predict whether a person’s annual income is above $50,000 or not based on personal and demographic attributes collected from a U.S. Census survey.
Link: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/2/adult

Some questions that we may ask: 
1. Is there a relationship between education level (education-num) and earning more than $50K?
2. How do hours worked per week differ between individuals earning above and below $50K?
3. Which occupations are most associated with higher income?
4. How does age relate to the likelihood of earning more than $50K?
5. Are there noticeable income differences by gender in the dataset?
6. How does income inequality appear across racial groups within the same occupation categories?
